## Basis and limits

- `GOAL-TRIGGER-001` alone owns signed-trigger, outbox and inbox semantics (CA guidance 2026-10-15, item 2). This register records TRON's and HELIOS's assumptions against it. It defines no alternative contract.
- The contract is not an authority yet: its PID candidate is on a local branch (TRG@64c843b, `pids/GOAL-TRIGGER-001.md`), its backlog item has no `pid_path` (B17), and its own contradiction register marks blockers T-1, T-2, T-6, T-7, T-8, T-10, T-11 as open. "Covered" below therefore means "covered by the candidate as drafted", not "frozen". The register must be refreshed when the contract exists (lane PID, open decision 5).
- Holders: D1 (D1@37d5f00), T1C (TRON-T1 candidate, T1C@3f14c5e), T4 (`TRON-T4`: backlog entry at GOAL@ceaf029 `backlog/backlog.yaml` and H2@2780076 section 1), H3 (`HELIOS-T3`: backlog entry and H2@2780076 F-3; no PID exists), FALCON (documents cited through H2 and TRG, not read here).
- Status vocabulary: `covered` (the candidate states the same thing or a compatible rule), `contradicted` (the candidate states a different rule), `unspecified` (the candidate is silent, or leaves it to an open decision, or the holder has not specified it).
