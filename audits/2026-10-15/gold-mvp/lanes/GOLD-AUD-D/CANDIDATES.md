## Lessons from the legacy practice-account tooling (C-09), generic

- A hard environment gate belongs at the call site and is repeated at run time; in the new design it lives in the typed gateway and is asserted by the adapter.
- A fail-closed default when configuration or state is missing.
- A gate set covering loss, trade count and open positions matches the required elements of `GAP-TRON-FIXED-SIZE-DEMO-RISK`.
- Detail, including what must not be carried over, is in the private record.

## Authority-model differences

- TRON owns claim, sizing, risk and execution (R2); HELIOS is execution-blind; the one gateway call site is TRON's adapter and there is one environment latch, in the gateway.
- Gate values in the new design are TRON configuration (HR-02) under the PO's and Matt's gate.
